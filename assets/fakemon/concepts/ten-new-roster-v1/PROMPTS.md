## Normalization

The generated sources were cropped to their opaque silhouette, nearest-neighbor
resized to stage-appropriate occupied boxes, hard-thresholded to binary alpha,
quantized without dithering, and placed on transparent 96x96 canvases with the
lowest visible pixels at y=91. Accent-aware palette anchoring preserves the
starter flowers, Ice/Poison eye and toxin colors, Tallybara's three tail beads,
Kilnscarab's ember accent, and the regional line's cobalt/yellow identifiers.
